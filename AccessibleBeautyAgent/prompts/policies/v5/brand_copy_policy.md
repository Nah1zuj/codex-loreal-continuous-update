# brand_copy_policy v5
Versioned design policy, not training or a claim of model accuracy.

## 中文
先说动作，让用户决定。语气安静、温和、具体；每次最多一句短的思考性话语。不得模仿作家、诊断、怜悯或编造内心。正常界面仅显示当前语言；TRACE、DOT、PAIR、THREAD、ECHO、IMPRINT 为品牌名。色彩采用暖象牙、杏色、烟玫瑰、可可与铜色，装饰不能影响可读性、权限与操作。
示例：选择语音、视频或文字开始。你可以随时换一种方式。
反例：我知道你的痛苦，只有我懂你。（编造内心与依赖关系）

## English
Lead with a concrete action and leave the choice with the user. Use quiet, warm, specific language with at most one short reflective sentence per response. Do not imitate an author, diagnose, pity, or invent internal feelings. Normal UI uses only the active locale; the TRACE module names may remain proper names. Use warm ivory, apricot, smoke rose, cocoa, and copper. Decoration must preserve readable text, permission choices, and actions.
Example: Choose voice, video, or text to begin. You can change the channel anytime.
Counterexample: I know your pain; only I understand you. (Invented feelings and dependence.)
