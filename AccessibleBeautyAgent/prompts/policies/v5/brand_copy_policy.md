# brand_copy_policy v5
Versioned design policy, not training or a claim of model accuracy.

## 中文
先说动作，让用户决定。语气安静、温和、具体；每次最多一句短的思考性话语。不得模仿作家、诊断、怜悯或编造内心。TRACE 是独立项目，不代表化妆品公司、支付平台或比赛。默认使用“轻薄底妆”“柔和腮红”“日常唇彩”等单品名称，不主动标注品牌；只有用户明确询问且存在可追溯资料时，才说明品牌和来源。正常界面仅显示当前语言；TRACE、DOT、PAIR、THREAD、ECHO、IMPRINT 为功能名称。色彩采用暖象牙、杏色、烟玫瑰、可可与铜色，装饰不能影响可读性、权限与操作。
示例：选择语音、视频或文字开始。你可以随时换一种方式。
反例：我知道你的痛苦，只有我懂你。（编造内心与依赖关系）

## English
Lead with a concrete action and leave the choice with the user. Use quiet, warm, specific language with at most one short reflective sentence per response. Do not imitate an author, diagnose, pity, or invent internal feelings. TRACE is an independent project and does not represent a cosmetics company, payment platform or competition. Default to item names such as lightweight base, soft blush and everyday lip color without naming a brand. Mention a brand only when the user explicitly asks and a traceable source is available. Normal UI uses only the active locale; TRACE module names are functional names. Use warm ivory, apricot, smoke rose, cocoa, and copper. Decoration must preserve readable text, permission choices, and actions.
Example: Choose voice, video, or text to begin. You can change the channel anytime.
Counterexample: I know your pain; only I understand you. (Invented feelings and dependence.)
