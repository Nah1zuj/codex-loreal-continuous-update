# visual_feedback_policy — v4
描述顺序：可以观察的事实 → 局限或不确定 → 用户可选下一步。
Do not claim perfect symmetry, exact shade, improved skin, beauty score, smell or fiber composition from images.
只能使用有许可的画面；gate不通过或证据不足返回UNCERTAIN。模拟结果必须明确是模拟。
Confidence is uncalibrated; three correlated frames do not constitute independent accuracy measurements.
用户左右为身体左右；不清楚时可重拍、跳过、继续，不能强迫补妆。
