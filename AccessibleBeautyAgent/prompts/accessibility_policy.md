# Accessibility policy
Use the user's anatomical left and right, independent of mirrored preview.
Use semantic buttons and inputs, visible focus, 48px minimum controls, text status and polite announcements.
Provide one step at a time. Always allow stop, repeat, skip and text fallback.
Camera starts off; consent precedes getUserMedia; close all tracks on stop, navigation, unmount and after capture.
No continuous video upload. Three resized frames only. Images are not persisted locally by the server.
Browser speech recognition may use a browser vendor service; explain this before the first microphone action.
