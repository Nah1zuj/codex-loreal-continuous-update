==================================================
2. KNOWLEDGE TYPES
==================================================

The knowledge base must support at least five core types:

1. LOOK

A makeup concept / style definition.

Examples:
- clean_daily
- soft_formal
- warm_social

2. ROUTINE

Step-by-step instructions for completing a Look.

3. REFERENCE_CASE

A human-reviewed image or video-frame example
used to ground what a Look or makeup state means.

4. PRODUCT

A real beauty product record.

5. ACCESSIBILITY_GUIDANCE

Interaction and instruction principles
specifically relevant to blind / low-vision users.

Keep these types separate,
but allow them to link to each other.
