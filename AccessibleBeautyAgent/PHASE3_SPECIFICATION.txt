==================================================
2. CORE BRAND PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

The product philosophy is NOT:

“AI becomes the eyes of blind people.”

Avoid this framing.

The product philosophy is:

Beauty information is commonly designed
to be accessed visually.

When sight is not the most reliable information channel,
beauty information does not disappear.

It can be translated into:

voice
language
touch
rhythm
structured guidance
memory

Core idea:

Information does not disappear when sight does.
It changes form.

Use this idea as conceptual inspiration,
but DO NOT present it as a direct quotation
from any literary work unless exact citation rights
and wording are verified.
